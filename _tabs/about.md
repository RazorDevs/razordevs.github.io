## RazorDevs

The Razor Devs group was founded back in 2022 by 345boneshoss, Aethyus, darksonic300 and TunefulTurnip, aiming to develop Deep Aether, only a few months old at the time. The growth of Deep Aether in particular led to the release of AeroBlender, one of the most popular addons for the Aether to this day, due to its many worldgen utilities.
